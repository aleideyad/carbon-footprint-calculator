Climate change is real!!

Climate change is one of the biggest challenges facing our planet today. Rising temperatures, melting ice caps and extreme weather are all linked to the greenhouse gases released by human activity. Every day, the way we travel, the energy we use and the food we eat adds to the carbon dioxide in our atmosphere.

Do you know how much carbon you produce? Assess your carbon footprint!

Small changes in our daily lives can make a big difference. By understanding your carbon footprint, you can take simple steps to protect the environment for future generations.
